# NotCo: Company Research Report

Nov 18, 2026 · @Rey Riordan

## Summary

NotCo is a Chilean food-tech company, valued at $1.5B in 2021, that is now becoming an AI software company. It became famous for plant-based NotMayo, NotMilk and NotBurger made with its AI, Giuseppe. Since 2025 it has sold or shut almost all of its food operations outside Chile and bet on licensing Giuseppe to big food makers such as Kraft Heinz, Ferrero and Barry Callebaut.

| Fact | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Founded | 2015, Santiago, Chile; offices in San Francisco, Santiago, Buenos Aires, São Paulo |
| Founders | Matías Muchnick (CEO), Karim Pichara (CTO), Pablo Zamora (left 2020) |
| Funding | About $425–450M equity from Kaszek, The Craftory, Bezos Expeditions, L Catterton, Future Positive, Tiger Global, Princeville and others |
| Last valuation | $1.5B (Series D, July 2021; reaffirmed December 2022) |
| Revenue | About $65–75M in 2025 (estimates), down from about $100M in 2022; roughly half from AI |
| Units | NotCo AI: enterprise formulation software and services, said to be profitable. NotCo Foods: consumer brand, now run directly only in Chile |
| Staff | Peaked at roughly 400–450; cut in 2022, 2024 and 2026 |

**The take.** NotCo's AI-first pivot looks real: named enterprise clients, claimed 70–80% gross margins, and a $30M contract won against big AI vendors. But the numbers are self-reported, total revenue has fallen, and the $1.5B valuation dates from the plant-based boom. The next 12 months should show whether the AI business can stand on its own and what becomes of the last food market, Chile.

**How it started.** After selling Eggless, Muchnick went to UC Berkeley's biochemistry department and saw that pharma used computation to discover formulations while food R&D still ran on trial and error ([TechCrunch, 2018](https://techcrunch.com/2018/07/28/the-not-company-is-looking-to-start-a-food-revolution-from-chile)). He recruited Pichara at Harvard, then Zamora from UC Davis ([SOSV](https://sosv.com/the-ai-that-powers-plant-based-food-darling-notco/)).

Pichara and Zamora built the machine-learning engine, Giuseppe, named after the painter Giuseppe Arcimboldo, who composed faces from vegetables. The founders chose Santiago over the US because it was cheaper and research talent was as strong. NotMayo, a chickpea-based mayonnaise, was the first product.

Catriel Giuliano replaced Zamora as Chief R&D Officer ([La Tercera](https://www.latercera.com/pulso/noticia/notco-por-que-ya-not/)). In 2022 Muchnick told [Forbes](https://forbes.pe/editors-pick/2022-11-09/nuestra-revista-notco-nos-sentimos-como-el-apple-de-los-alimentos/) NotCo felt like "the Apple of food"; Pichara now says that "from design, we are an AI company" ([AgFunderNews](https://agfundernews.com/notco-cto-within-three-years-the-bulk-of-our-revenue-will-be-from-the-b2b-business-from-design-we-are-an-ai-company)).

## Technology: Giuseppe and NotCo AI

Giuseppe, a formulation model trained on about ten years of NotCo's own molecular, sensory and consumer data, is the company's core asset. Since 2025 it is sold to other food companies as NotCo AI, which management calls profitable and which now drives the strategy.

**How Giuseppe works**

1. A brief comes in: match a target product such as cow's milk, or reformulate one (cut sugar, swap cocoa, remove synthetic dyes).
2. The model links food's molecular makeup to human perception of taste, texture, smell and color, then searches thousands of plant and other ingredients for combinations that hit the target ([FoodNavigator](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2021/10/21/NotCo-founder-Why-are-we-doing-a-barista-version-of-plant-based-milk-There-s-no-barista-version-of-dairy-milk/)).
3. It now also weighs cost, nutrition, manufacturability and regulatory compliance ([Forbes, Mar 2026](https://forbes.cl/negocios/2026-03-23/notco-esta-ayudando-a-conglomerados-alimentarios-a-preparar-sus-productos-para-el-futuro-a-traves-de-la-ia/)).
4. Chefs and food scientists cook and test prototypes and feed the results back; the system generates hundreds to thousands of recipes a month ([New Hope](https://www.newhope.com/people-and-company-profiles/new-milk-machine-ai-formulations-lead-not-company)).

The best-known output is NotMilk, which pairs pineapple and cabbage because together they produce lactones, the aroma compounds of dairy ([AgFunderNews](https://agfundernews.com/notco-cto-within-three-years-the-bulk-of-our-revenue-will-be-from-the-b2b-business-from-design-we-are-an-ai-company)). NotCo holds 31 patents, 13 of them US patents on the AI (Forbes).

**What NotCo AI sells**

- **Platform:** Giuseppe with modules named Concept, Discovery and Elevate ([notco.ai](https://notco.ai/about)); Concept Quant, a 2025 portal that turns briefs into market-ready concepts; Giuseppe World, a synthetic-consumer simulator ([DF](https://www.df.cl/df-lab/tech-empresas/notco-despide-a-parte-de-los-equipos-cientifico-y-culinario-para-priorizar)); a generative fragrance formulator launched in 2024.
- **Delivery:** tailored programs in which NotCo staff work inside the client's R&D ([La Tercera](https://www.latercera.com/pulso/noticia/notco-por-que-ya-not/)), moving toward enterprise installs in the client's own cloud with its proprietary data ([FEN U. de Chile](https://fen.uchile.cl/?p=34542)).
- **Use cases:** mostly not plant-based any more; a NotCo executive put that at 90% of B2B requests in 2025 ([Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/16/05/2025/notco-vertical-b2b-ia/)). Typical work: cocoa substitution, sugar reduction, replacing synthetic dyes (50+ active color projects per [The Spoon](https://thespoon.tech/?p=57651)), GLP-1-era reformulation, fragrance and scent profiling.
- **Named clients and partners:** Kraft Heinz, PepsiCo, Mondelēz, Mars, Ferrero, [Barry Callebaut](https://www.barry-callebaut.com/system/files/2025-11/Press%20Release%20-%20Barry%20Callebaut%20partners%20with%20NotCo%20AI%20to%20unlock%20next-level%20chocolate%20innovation.pdf), The Magnum Ice Cream Company and Bimbo. Coca-Cola, Danone and Unilever requested projects after the Kraft Heinz deal.

## Challenges

The consumer business never reached sustained profitability, its category shrank, and repeated cuts have left a much smaller company still carrying a 2021 valuation of $1.5B.

- **A shrinking category.** US retail sales of plant-based meat and seafood fell 10% to $1B in 2025, and plant milk fell 2% to $2.7B ([GFI via AgFunderNews](https://agfundernews.com/us-alt-meat-slump-continues-but-plant-protein-gains-traction-in-beverages-gfi)). Global plant-based retail grew 3% to $28.9B, led by Europe.
- **Revenue below its peak.** Sales peaked near $100M in 2022 and were about $65–75M in 2025 (PitchBook via La Tercera; Forbes estimate), roughly half of it from AI.
- **Missed profitability targets.** In January 2024 Muchnick said Chile and Argentina would turn profitable that year. Targets then moved to EBITDA break-even in Q1 2025, profit in 2026, then 2027; La Tercera reports none was met. Muchnick said in April 2026 the consumer business had reached break-even; the accounts are private, so neither claim can be checked.
- **Rolling cuts.** About 6% (32 people) in August 2022 ([La República](https://www.larepublica.co/globoeconomia/notco-despide-el-6-de-su-plantilla-global-por-el-complejo-escenario-economico-3426865)), 11% in December 2024, and 16 managers in July 2026, from a peak of roughly 400–450 staff. Former staff say new product development fell from about ten a year to two, and no team now does new research.
- **Legal hit at home.** The May 2026 Supreme Court ruling bars "milk" wording and cow imagery in Chile, its last directly run market, and forced a withdrawal of affected packs.
- **Valuation overhang.** $1.5B is about 20 times 2025 revenue. Muchnick told Forbes he needs no new capital and that AI multiples would justify a higher price; a priced round would test that.
- **A crowded AI field.** NotCo competes for CPG AI budgets with Palantir, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic (Muchnick's own list) and with clients' in-house teams. Its edge rests on a proprietary data moat that outsiders cannot easily verify.

## Where they're going

NotCo is turning itself into an enterprise AI vendor for consumer-goods companies, with the NotCo food brand kept alive mainly through partners. The company says its aim is to make NotCo AI "the undisputed leader in AI applied to mass consumption, worldwide" ([DF](https://www.df.cl/df-lab/tech-empresas/notco-despide-a-parte-de-los-equipos-cientifico-y-culinario-para-priorizar)).

&#91;embedded content: NotCo business model, 2021 vs. 2026 · drawn from the sources in this report\]

In 2021 Giuseppe existed to feed NotCo's own brand and country teams; in 2026 it is the product, and the brand rides on partners.

**Stated direction**

- **AI first.** A source close to the company describes the goal as a "100% technology" company that keeps its food assets only if they turn a profit. The site, team cuts and messaging all now center on NotCo AI.
- **Brand through partners.** Kraft Heinz sells the brand in North America, Molinos in Argentina and Uruguay, Ferrara in Brazil. NotCo keeps Chile as a showcase for what Giuseppe can make (Forbes).
- **From formulation tool to "operating system."** Muchnick pitches Giuseppe as software that could run most decisions across innovation, marketing, sales and supply chain, installed in each client's cloud ([Forbes](https://forbes.cl/negocios/2026-03-23/notco-esta-ayudando-a-conglomerados-alimentarios-a-preparar-sus-productos-para-el-futuro-a-traves-de-la-ia/); [FEN](https://fen.uchile.cl/?p=34542)).
- **Beyond food.** NotCo now calls itself the "first foundational AI platform for the consumer goods industry" ([Barry Callebaut release](https://www.barry-callebaut.com/system/files/2025-11/Press%20Release%20-%20Barry%20Callebaut%20partners%20with%20NotCo%20AI%20to%20unlock%20next-level%20chocolate%20innovation.pdf)), with work in fragrance, scent profiling for a large pet company, dye replacement and synthetic-consumer testing.
- **No new money planned.** Muchnick says the AI unit's margins mean no fundraise is needed in the next few years.

**Open questions to watch**

1. Does Chile stay? Press reports a year-end 2026 deadline for the Chilean food unit to show profit; a sale or closure would complete the exit from running a food business.
2. Can AI revenue carry the company? Roughly half of $65–75M in 2025 sales came from AI. Growth must outrun the shrinking food line.
3. Is the data moat real? NotCo's pitch rests on ten years of proprietary formulation and sensory data. Large general-purpose AI vendors, and clients' own teams, are chasing the same budgets.
4. What happens to the Kraft Heinz tie-up as Kraft Heinz restructures? The JV's current form is not publicly documented.
5. How deep is the bench? After the 2024–2026 cuts, most technical leadership rests with Muchnick, Pichara, Menéndez and Giuliano.
6. Will the $1.5B valuation hold if the company raises again, seeks a buyer, or lists?
